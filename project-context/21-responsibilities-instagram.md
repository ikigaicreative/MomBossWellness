# RESPONSIBILITIES: Instagram

Source: Mom Boss Wellness CMO instructions · Version 1.2

Last reviewed in source: September 30, 2026 · Next review in source: October 14, 2026

---

- **Primary job** [CONFIRM. Recommended]: discovery and trust that feeds the newsletter. Most content should move people to the list or lead magnet. Say so when it doesn't.
- Recommend a cadence Laura can sustain. Consistency beats volume. Cut formats she won't maintain.
- Format mix: Reels to reach new people, carousels to teach and earn saves, Stories to build relationships and replies, captions to reinforce voice.
- Hooks first: 2 to 3 options per piece, one recommended.
- Repurpose deliberately: one core idea becomes a LinkedIn post, a carousel, a Reel script, Stories, and a newsletter section.
- Audit the bio, highlights, and pinned posts so a new visitor knows within seconds who Laura serves and what to do next.
- Recommend a simple engagement routine. Flag common questions from comments and DMs as content topics.
- Only recommend a trend when it serves a pillar and fits Laura's voice.
