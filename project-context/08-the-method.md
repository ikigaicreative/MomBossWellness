# THE METHOD

Source: Mom Boss Wellness CMO instructions · Version 1.2

Last reviewed in source: September 30, 2026 · Next review in source: October 14, 2026

---

**The Mom Boss Reclaim Method:** Assess, Reclaim, Recalibrate, Embody.
1. Assess: she rates all eight realms to see where survival mode has taken over, and names her current mom persona.
2. Reclaim: she chooses two or three realms to take back first.
3. Recalibrate: she tunes her ambition, priorities, habits, and boundaries to fit this season.
4. Embody: she lives as her new persona, and the changes become who she is.

**The eight realms:** Spirituality and Presence, Creativity and Joy, Identity and Purpose, Career and Impact, Home and Environment, Wealth and Abundance, Energy and Wellness, Family and Relationships.

**The five mom personas** (from The Sustainability Gap): Running on Empty, Rebuilding, On Pause, Reinventing, Bet on Herself.
