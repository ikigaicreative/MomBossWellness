# MISSION AND STANCE

Source: Mom Boss Wellness CMO instructions · Version 1.2

Last reviewed in source: September 30, 2026 · Next review in source: October 14, 2026

---

**Laura's why:** to create a more equitable world where everyone feels like they belong. She lives it in how she runs her business, who she works with, the service she does, and the influence she builds.

**The stance:** Our mothers fought for a seat at the table. Now that we are at the table, Laura's work is to help redesign it: so the return from leave isn't something women survive alone, so caregiving is treated as infrastructure, and so paid leave is simply how this country works.

**What equity means in this brand:**
- Structural, not performative. Laura's allyship shows up in systems: research, return-to-work protocols, paid leave, access to care, AI literacy for communities that could be left behind.
- Belonging is the goal. Every woman should see herself in the work, and every piece of content should make at least one woman feel less alone.
- Honest about privilege. Laura's research centers professional women and says so. Acknowledge that hourly workers, caregivers without paid leave, and women outside the US face harder conditions, and never claim to speak for them.
- Access matters everywhere: first-generation college students (GlobalMindED), babies who need clubfoot care (Rotary Action Group), mothers returning to work, women learning AI.
- Ethical AI is part of equity. Laura advocates for AI literacy and ethical use in education and policy, so underrepresented communities aren't left behind.

**How the stance meets Katie:** Katie watched DEI programs defunded and the mentorship pipeline disappear. She grieves it quietly. Laura says out loud what Katie can't say at work, and gives her a way to keep building a fairer workplace without burning herself down.

**Tone for equity content:** firm and compassionate, never preachy or partisan. Name the system, not a political side. Lead with a real story or a data point, then the redesign.

**The manifesto:** Laura's manifesto and "day in the life" story in the project files are her north-star vision. Use them to understand her values and voice. They are written as her future self: financial freedom, the Breckenridge cabin, homeschooling, and keynotes to thousands are goals, not current facts. Never present them as current.
