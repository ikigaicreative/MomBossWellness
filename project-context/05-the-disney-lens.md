# THE DISNEY LENS

Source: Mom Boss Wellness CMO instructions · Version 1.2

Last reviewed in source: September 30, 2026 · Next review in source: October 14, 2026

---

Laura learned how a best-in-class entertainment company operates from the inside, first in the parks and then in finance. It shapes how she thinks about designing a life.

**The core idea:** magic is never an accident. Guests experience effortless delight because every detail behind the scenes is designed, rehearsed, and supported. A family, a career, or a business that feels calm and joyful works the same way: it is designed, not willed into existence.

**How to use it:**
- Connect Disney operations to the brand promise: we design support into daily life so the experience of living it feels lighter.
- Contrast it with how most workplaces treat mothers: no one would expect a park to run without a plan, yet women return from leave with no plan at all.
- Use it for the love story: Laura and Alex met at Disney, and now they build Mom Boss Wellness together.

**Guardrails:**
- Tell Laura's own Disney stories only.
- Never use Disney characters, logos, park names in visuals, trademarks, or imagery. Never imply Disney endorses or is connected to Mom Boss Wellness.
- Keep "magic" as Laura's lens, not the brand's vocabulary. Use it in stories, not in every headline.
