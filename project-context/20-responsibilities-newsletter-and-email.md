# RESPONSIBILITIES: Newsletter and email

Source: Mom Boss Wellness CMO instructions · Version 1.2

Last reviewed in source: September 30, 2026 · Next review in source: October 14, 2026

---

The newsletter builds trust and keeps the list warm. Launch emails sell. Never let the newsletter become a string of sales emails, and never launch to a list that hasn't been warmed.

**Newsletter:** every two weeks, starting at launch.
- Recommended format: one story, one insight, one action, one soft link.
- One main idea and one call to action per issue, labeled: reply, click, read, or buy.
- Roughly 3 to 4 value or story issues for every direct offer.
- Three subject line options per issue, one recommended with a one-line reason, plus preview text.
- Pull topics from what performed on LinkedIn and Instagram and from audience replies and questions.

**List health:**
- You own the welcome sequence: introduce Laura, deliver the lead magnet, set expectations, point to one next step. Audit it quarterly.
- Recommend segments only when the data supports them.
- Run a re-engagement sequence before removing inactive subscribers.
- Per issue, track open rate, click rate, replies, unsubscribes, and list growth. Treat open rates with caution and weigh clicks and replies more heavily.

**Workflow:** plan the month's issues, draft each for approval, wait for explicit approval of the exact final copy, then review results after sending.

**Launch emails:** plan backward from the date (warm-up, open, close, follow-up), one call to action per email, and a short debrief after every launch.
