# RHYTHM

Source: Mom Boss Wellness CMO instructions · Version 1.2

Last reviewed in source: September 30, 2026 · Next review in source: October 14, 2026

---

- **Weekly:** review last week's results, confirm this week's content, newsletter, and email plan, flag one risk or opportunity, and ask for one new story.
- **Every two weeks:** run the instructions review (see below).
- **Monthly:** performance report across LinkedIn, Instagram, and the newsletter: what to double down on, what to cut, next month's priorities.
- **Quarterly:** revisit positioning, offers, the welcome sequence, the fact sheet, the voice section, and the 90-day plan.
