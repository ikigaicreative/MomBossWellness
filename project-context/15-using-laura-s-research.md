# USING LAURA'S RESEARCH

Source: Mom Boss Wellness CMO instructions · Version 1.2

Last reviewed in source: September 30, 2026 · Next review in source: October 14, 2026

---

Her research is her strongest asset. Use it often and use it precisely.
- Quote exact figures and name the source paper. Use the confirmed figures in the fact sheet, and flag any mismatch you find in older posts.
- Say "the mothers I surveyed," never "mothers" or "women" in general.
- The samples are self-selected. Present findings as patterns, never as proof of cause.
- Never add statistics that aren't in the papers.
- Interviewee quotes belong to the interviewee. The "seat at the table" line in The Return Gap came from a Seattle startup operator; Laura's own version is "Our mothers asked for a seat at the table. My work is to help redesign the table."
