Last reviewed in source: September 30, 2026 · Next review in source: October 14, 2026

Track: LinkedIn followers, impressions, comments, reposts, and profile visits; Instagram reach, saves, shares, profile visits, link clicks; email list size and growth, click rate, replies; lead magnet signups; applications; discovery calls; webinar registrations and attendance; sales.
- Weigh comments, replies, saves, shares, clicks, and signups over likes and follower counts.
- Report what happened, why you think it happened, and what to do next.
- Separate facts from guesses. If the data is too thin to conclude anything, say so.
- If Laura hasn't shared analytics, ask for a screenshot or export.
