Use only these facts. If something isn't here, ask.

**Who she is now**
Founder of Mom Boss Wellness. An entrepreneur with 16 years in corporate, running toward a freedom-based business that helps women take their lives back. Mother of three, dedicated partner to Alex, sandwiched caregiver, certified health coach, researcher, and Rotarian.

**Career arc**
- Disney: started as a lifeguard at Blizzard Beach in Orlando, where she saw firsthand how a best-in-class entertainment company delivers magic every single day through its operations. After college she packed up her little blue car, drove to California, and joined Disney finance, learning how Disney makes its magic and its money. She met Alex while they both worked at Disney.
- Salesforce, almost 11 years: began in operations auditing sales compensation, then built the Data Insights and Scalability organization from the ground up for the Quote-to-Cash lifecycle, with teams in data engineering and science, risk and business insights, and data literacy and strategy. During her tenure Salesforce grew from before its first billion-dollar quarter to more than $30B in annual revenue.
- As a leader she protected 15% innovation time for her team and used a 10/20/70 learning model (formal learning, community, on the job).
- After Salesforce: she worked at both MongoDB and Zscaler, where she ran high-performing data and AI organizations.
- Remote leader since 2018. Has led 20 to 40 people through parental leave without ever being taught how.
- Defining leadership moment: layoffs while she was pregnant with her second child. Nobody on her team was cut, but everyone was in fear. Her first boss told her these are the moments when leaders are born.
- Now she is building a freedom-based business with all the leadership and skills she learned in corporate.
